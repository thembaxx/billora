1. Status and service

These are proposed terms for review of Billora’s free browser-local preview, including its installed PWA. The operator’s legal identity, address and contact details are pending confirmation. This document is visibly a draft, not a completed production contract. It does not claim that opening the preview constitutes acceptance of undisclosed future charges or integrations.

Billora helps you organise municipal statements, properties, personal notes and spending summaries. It is independent of municipalities, banks and collection providers. It does not act as a municipality, payment collector, credit bureau or professional financial, legal or tax adviser.

2. Your records and authority

Use the app only for lawful purposes and for records you own or are authorised to manage. You remain responsible for the authority and lawful basis for using a tenant’s, owner’s or other person’s information, and for keeping any copies you export secure. The preview offers no landlord/tenant permission controls or shared cloud workspace.

Do not use the app to obtain unauthorised account access, impersonate another person, upload malicious files or attack this website or third-party systems. Do not enter municipal passwords, payment-card details or genuine credentials into the demo.

You retain your rights in your documents and records. Using the organiser does not transfer ownership of your bills to Billora or grant a right to sell your personal information. Third-party software and assets remain subject to their applicable licences.

3. Figures, reminders and insights

Sample data and demo responses are illustrative. Imported balances and due dates are based on records you review and enter; they are not verified live municipal balances. PDF extraction can miss or misread text and does not validate the bill. Check the original statement and the official municipal account before taking action.

Spending comparisons are informational and do not establish a billing error. A personal “marked paid” note is not proof of settlement. Downloaded query letters are drafts, not lodged disputes. Calendar reminders require you to import a downloaded ICS file into a calendar; Billora does not send background push reminders. You remain responsible for checking due dates, arranging payment and submitting queries through official channels.

4. Municipal links and demonstrations

Payment links open an external official portal. Billora does not take money, store payment credentials, submit a payment instruction or confirm reconciliation. The third-party portal’s terms, charges and privacy practices apply to your interaction there.

The demo cloud account, authorisation checkbox, permissions, sync and balances are local simulations. They do not establish a real cloud account, municipal partnership or legally operative authority with a municipality. Read the integration disclaimer for the exact boundaries.

5. Availability, storage and changes

The preview is currently free and does not contain paid subscriptions or payment collection. Any future paid service would need its price and applicable terms presented before you agree to it.

Features and offline/native capabilities vary by browser. Updates, storage limits, browser resets, access changes or withdrawal of the preview can affect availability. Keep independent copies of records you need; the app offers no server recovery or availability guarantee. The data policy explains what exports do and do not contain.

Material changes require updated notices and, where appropriate, terms or permission before the new feature begins. Existing demo actions do not authorise future processing.

6. Statutory rights and applicable law

Nothing in these proposed terms excludes or limits a right, remedy, duty or liability that applicable law does not allow to be excluded or limited, including applicable South African consumer and privacy protections. No blanket waiver, mandatory private arbitration or indemnity is imposed by this draft.

The proposed governing law is South African law, subject to mandatory laws that apply to you and your rights to approach an appropriate court or regulator. This wording does not restrict the Information Regulator’s powers. An enforceable allocation of liability and the final operator-specific terms remain for review before production launch.
