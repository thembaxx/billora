1. What the demo does

The screens marked DEMO INTEGRATION demonstrate a possible future account and municipal-connection experience. They save a demo profile, chosen property and simulated authorisation/sync timestamps locally in this browser. Use a made-up name and email; no password is requested and no verification email is sent.

Sync shows fixed example balances, references and statement counts. Waiting indicators and timestamps are interface simulations. They do not show a request to, or response from, a municipality or cloud provider.

2. The checkbox is not real municipal consent

The demo checkbox records a local acknowledgement for the demonstration. It does not validate your identity or property rights, grant provider OAuth/API access, appoint Billora as your agent, create a collection mandate or lodge consent with a municipality. It does not establish a provider partnership or satisfy a future production integration’s authorisation requirements.

Do not enter municipal usernames, passwords, API credentials or payment details. No Billora cloud authentication, cloud statement storage or cross-device sync is active in this release.

3. Keep demo results separate from real records

Demo balances never update your imported statements or personal payment notes. A downloadable demo response is marked demo: true, describes its simulated source, and records liveMunicipalConnection: false, cloudStorage: false and paymentProcessing: false.

Do not use demo balances or statement counts as evidence of a debt, payment, dispute or reconciliation. Use your original statement and official account channel for decisions. Disconnect and reset controls remove local demo records only; they revoke no real municipal connection because none exists.

4. Before any live integration

A live service would need an authorised provider arrangement, proper account authentication, scoped permissions, secure server processing, purpose and retention disclosures, and a real revocation process. Payment collection would require its own authorised payment and reconciliation arrangement.

Billora must identify the real provider, explain what will be retrieved or shared, and obtain any required authority before enabling such a feature. The demo does not pre-authorise those future actions.
