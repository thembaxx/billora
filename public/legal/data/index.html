1. What is stored, and for how long

This inventory describes Billora’s current app storage. Local records have no automatic expiry and remain until removed by you or your browser. This does not create a legal requirement for you to retain a bill indefinitely.

RecordLocationRemoval
Properties, statement figures, extracted text and payment notesbillora-workspace in localStorageDelete a statement or clear workspace

Downloaded files and any browser or device backups are outside these stores. Hosting delivery/access records are separate; their retention is pending provider confirmation and is not controlled by Clear workspace.

2. Make a copy before deleting

  1. Open Settings and choose Export account data (JSON). This includes property records, statement metadata, extracted text and personal payment notes.
  2. Open each imported statement and choose Original PDF to save the source document separately. The JSON export does not include binary PDF files.
  3. Save demo-response JSON separately if needed. Account exports do not include demo-profile state, preferences or app caches.

Exports may contain personal and financial information. Store them securely and share them only with intended recipients. Billora has no JSON restore/import feature in this release; an export is a readable record, not a guaranteed one-click workspace backup.

3. Delete statements or clear the workspace

To remove one statement, open it and choose Remove statement, then confirm. This removes its saved metadata and original PDF from this app’s local stores.

For all property and statement records, go to Settings → Your workspace → Clear workspace & start fresh and confirm Clear workspace. This removes sample and imported records, property records and saved original PDFs. It does not remove your preferences, welcome-tour state, demo profile or demo connections.

For the demo, open Explore cloud & municipal demo in Settings. Disconnect demo removes one simulated connection; Reset demo account & connections removes the local demo profile and all demo connections.

4. Remove all website data

Use your browser’s site-data or website-storage controls for this Billora origin to remove localStorage, IndexedDB and cached app files. Depending on the browser, you may also need to remove the service worker or installed app. Uninstalling a PWA alone may leave website data behind. Remove downloaded exports and PDFs separately if you no longer need them.

Clearing local site data may also remove this website’s access/session information and offline support. Reopening an empty Billora profile displays illustrative sample accounts again; those examples are not recovered personal data.

Deletion from the app is a logical removal from its stores. Billora cannot guarantee forensic erasure of device storage, erase operating-system backups, remove copies you shared, or delete a municipality’s source records. We cannot remotely restore or wipe your browser-local records.

5. Keep records only as long as needed

Review your records when a tenancy, management mandate or property relationship ends. Keep only what you are lawfully entitled or required to retain. Your own tax, contract or statutory duties may affect retention; Billora does not determine those duties for you.

Before operator-controlled storage is introduced, a purpose-based schedule, lawful retention exceptions, deletion/backup processes and provider retention periods must be documented. POPIA section 14 generally limits retention to what is necessary, subject to its exceptions; this draft does not invent a blanket five- or seven-year retention period.
