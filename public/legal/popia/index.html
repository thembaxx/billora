1. Your POPIA rights

Subject to the Act’s conditions and exceptions, you may ask whether a responsible party holds your personal information, request access and information about recipients, request correction or deletion, object to qualifying processing, withdraw consent where relied upon, and submit a complaint. Withdrawal does not undo lawful processing that has already occurred. Certain solely automated decisions are also regulated by POPIA; Billora does not make credit or eligibility decisions.

These rights concern the processing in question. Billora cannot amend a municipality’s billing system or decide a municipal dispute. Direct requests about the municipality’s own records to that municipality as well.

2. Act on records in this browser

  • Access: view records in Statements and Properties; export account JSON from Settings and download original PDFs from each imported statement.
  • Correction: the preview does not have a general record editor. Remove an incorrect statement and import a reviewed replacement. Service-request drafts can be edited. Submitted-case notes and statuses remain clearly labelled as your own records. If you need to rebuild property records, export first, clear the workspace, and re-add the correct details.
  • Deletion: remove one statement or service request, remove draft evidence, clear the workspace, reset demo information separately, or clear all website data. See the step-by-step data policy.

Local controls do not replace a formal request for information held by an operator or service provider. The Billora operator cannot remotely retrieve or delete records that exist only in your browser.

3. Request handling: contact pending

The legal operator, Information Officer and privacy-request email have not yet been supplied. No privacy-request form or monitored request inbox is offered in this preview. These details and a verified request-handling process must be in place before production launch; see publication details.

For a future operator request, identify the relevant service and right, describe the records involved, and give a safe reply method. Identity verification must be proportionate to the request. Do not send an entire municipal statement, password or identity document unless a secure, justified process requires it. Never post personal records in a public source-code issue.

Requests must be handled within the periods and requirements of applicable law. This draft does not invent an unsupported “30-day deletion guarantee”. Some retention or access exceptions may apply and must be explained when relied upon.

4. Information Regulator complaints and forms

You may approach South Africa’s Information Regulator about alleged unlawful processing. You do not need Billora’s permission to do so. Use the Regulator’s current guidance and complaint channels:

  • POPIA guidance, forms and complaint instructions (opens a new tab)
  • Official e-Services portal (opens a new tab)

The guidance includes Form 1 for objections, Form 2 for correction/deletion requests and Form 5 for POPIA complaints. The Regulator’s website is authoritative for current forms and submission requirements. A Billora demo authorisation is not one of these statutory forms.

5. PAIA and production readiness

This rights guide is not a PAIA section 51 manual, an Information Officer registration, or a substitute for operational compliance. An operator-specific PAIA manual and the applicable access-request procedure remain pending assessment and completion. The final operator must also document provider arrangements, lawful processing grounds, retention, security and incident-response procedures.
