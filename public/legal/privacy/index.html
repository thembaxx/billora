1. Who and what this covers

This privacy policy draft covers the current Billora web app and installed PWA: a municipal-account organiser for South Africa. “Billora” refers to this product. The legal operator, business address and privacy contact are pending confirmation. This draft is not a statement that Billora has been certified as POPIA compliant.

The app has no production account login, cloud document store or live municipal connection. The cloud-account and authorisation screens are explicitly labelled local demonstrations. Access controls on this private website belong to the hosting service and do not create a Billora cloud account.

2. Information you choose to use

  • Property and account records: property names, addresses, municipalities, account references and property types.
  • Statements: PDFs you select, filenames, extracted text, amounts, periods, due dates, category figures and personal payment notes. Original bills may contain further personal or financial information.
  • Service requests: issue descriptions, property/account snapshots, requested assistance, evidence checklists, photos or documents you attach, local activity notes, submission references and personal follow-up dates. These are user-recorded, not municipality-verified updates.
  • Preferences: your chosen first name, appearance, balance visibility, motion preference and welcome-tour state.
  • Demo information: a made-up display name and email, a local identifier, selected property, simulated permissions, authorisation and sync timestamps. Use fictional details.

Optional place maps and location: Opening the map loads visible map tiles from OpenStreetMap Foundation servers, which may receive IP address and approximate viewed area. Only pressing Find sends the entered place query to the public Nominatim search service; there is no automatic address lookup or autocomplete. Only pressing My location requests browser/device geolocation permission. Your precise device coordinates remain in memory until you explicitly choose to save a property pin; viewing nearby map tiles can nevertheless disclose the viewed geographic area to the tile provider. Chosen coordinates are then stored with your property in this browser, included in local exports and available until you delete the workspace. Map tiles and place search require internet, and the PWA does not download map regions for offline use. Do not enter municipal account numbers or confidential information in place-search queries. OpenStreetMap and Nominatim are external services with their own privacy practices and availability limits.

These records are stored in this browser profile. Billora does not send statement files, extracted text, service-request details, evidence files or demo-profile fields to an application server. Do not include unnecessary identity numbers, banking details, children’s information or special personal information.

3. Purposes and lawful processing

The app uses the records you select to organise properties, display reviewed bills, calculate spending summaries and evidence-linked comparisons, keep personal notes and produce downloads you request. Suggested PDF fields use local patterns and must be reviewed; alerts are numeric thresholds, not verified municipal billing errors. Service-request ZIPs contain a draft letter, case JSON, linked statement PDF when available and attached evidence; you choose what to submit through the official channel. PDF text extraction and field suggestions run locally; they are not sent to an AI service. Your preferences personalise the interface.

The intended grounds for operator-controlled processing are performance of the service you request where necessary for an agreement, and legitimate interests in secure, reliable website delivery, subject to POPIA’s requirements and your rights. A legal obligation may require specific processing. The operator must confirm the purpose-by-purpose lawful grounds before production launch. Opening this policy or continuing to use the preview is not blanket consent to unrelated uses, marketing or future integrations.

You can explore sample data without importing a real bill. Optional fields can be left blank. If you do not save property or statement details, the related organising features will not have those records to work with.

4. Hosting, recipients and transfers

Billora is hosted on third-party infrastructure, currently Vercel. Website delivery and access services may receive technical request information such as your IP address, browser, requested URL and request time. Hosting sign-in may use session cookies or similar technology. These services are separate from Billora’s local statement store; review the hosting provider’s applicable privacy information.

The operator has not yet confirmed the complete infrastructure-provider inventory, log-retention periods or processing locations. No South African data-residency guarantee is made. Before production, operator-controlled international transfers must be assessed under POPIA section 72, and applicable provider agreements and safeguards documented.

Billora has no built-in advertising, analytics SDK, data-sale feature or automated sharing of your account records. A download goes to the destination you choose. Calendar, share and municipal-portal services process information under their own policies when you choose to use them. Do not put account numbers or personal information in a page URL.

5. Retention, security and your control

Local records remain until you delete them, clear browser storage or the browser removes them. There is no automatic expiry or server recovery of your bills. The data policy explains exports, the separate workspace and demo resets, and how to clear all site data.

Browser storage is not a Billora-encrypted vault and has no per-user permissions within a shared browser profile. Hide balances changes the screen, not stored records. Use a trusted device, an appropriate screen lock and a separate browser profile; avoid real bills in shared or public profiles. HTTPS protects website delivery but does not encrypt your local records with a separate Billora key.

If a security compromise falls within the operator’s POPIA responsibilities, notification to the Regulator and affected people must follow section 22, including its requirement to notify as soon as reasonably possible after discovery, subject to the statutory conditions. This is not a promise of continuous local-device monitoring or a fixed response time.

6. Your rights and contact options

POPIA provides rights, subject to its conditions and lawful exceptions, to be informed, request access, request correction or deletion, object to qualifying processing, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, and complain to the Information Regulator. Billora’s spending charts do not make credit, legal or eligibility decisions about you.

See POPIA rights & requests for practical steps and official forms. The operator/privacy email is pending; this preview has no privacy-request submission form. The operator cannot retrieve records that exist only in your browser through a remote support request. Do not post bills or account references in public repository issues.

Billora is intended for adults and people with authority to manage the relevant property. It does not offer child accounts. Only use another person’s records where you have a lawful basis and appropriate authority.

7. Changes to this notice

The review date appears on this page. The policy must be revised before adding cloud storage, real account login, tracking, payments or live municipal retrieval. Any required notice, choice or consent must be provided before the new processing starts. A later policy cannot retroactively turn this demo into a live authorisation.
