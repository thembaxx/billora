1. App storage is not a cloud account

Billora uses localStorage for account metadata, preferences, the welcome-tour state and demo records. Original PDFs and service-request evidence use separate IndexedDB databases. These browser databases are separate from cookies and are tied to this website’s origin and browser profile. They do not automatically follow you to another device, browser or domain.

The app’s own code does not set cookies or install analytics or advertising trackers. Self-hosted fonts and assets load from the Billora website. This statement covers the Billora app code, not every hosting or sign-in service around it.

The data inventory lists the storage keys, purposes and removal controls. Local storage is used for the features you choose; it is not a separate encrypted vault.

2. Offline files and updates

A service worker caches the app shell and public assets so previously cached features can open offline. Legal pages are included in that public cache. Your original PDFs and request-evidence files are kept separately in IndexedDB; they are not uploaded into a hosting cache or copied into the app-shell cache.

Cache files can change when the app updates or when your browser removes storage. Offline availability is not a backup promise. Clearing website data can remove both the app cache and your saved records.

3. Hosting and external services

ChatGPT Sites and its access/sign-in infrastructure may use cookies, session storage and technical logs for delivery, authentication and security. Their exact current storage inventory, retention and processing locations are pending confirmation for the final operator notice. Refer to OpenAI’s privacy policy (opens a new tab) for that service’s practices.

Municipal portals, calendar apps and native sharing destinations have their own policies. Following an external link does not grant Billora access to that provider’s cookies or account. Your browser controls may affect both the preview’s access session and its local app storage.

4. Your choices

Manage appearance, motion and balance display in Settings. Use the separate workspace and demo resets, or your browser’s site-data controls, to remove stored records. Blocking all browser storage may stop persistence or offline features from working.

The app does not display an “accept all cookies” banner because its code does not currently add optional analytics or advertising storage. This does not certify the hosting layer as cookie-free. Any future non-essential tracking must be assessed, disclosed and given the choices or consent required before it starts. No consent to future tracking is inferred from using this preview.
